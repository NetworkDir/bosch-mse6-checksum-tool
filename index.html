ECU Checksum Fixer

Verify and repair ECU checksums after editing a map in TunerPro or WinOLS. Load a plain binary image (not a raw read that still has the flash tool's file header). Runs entirely in your browser: your file is never uploaded.

Drop an ECU image here or click to choose a file (.bin, .MOD, .dtf, ...)

Supported algorithms

    Advanced

    Scan ranges in hex. Only needed if your image keeps its checksum records somewhere else.

    For research, diagnostics and motorsport use. Flashing an ECU can immobilise or damage a vehicle, and modifying emissions-related calibration is illegal for road use in most jurisdictions. Keep an untouched backup of the original image. You are responsible for what you flash.
